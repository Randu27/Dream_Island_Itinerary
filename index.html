Experience the true beauty of Sri Lanka on this unforgettable 7-day journey, where nature, culture, adventure, and relaxation come together. Explore ancient heritage sites, enjoy an exciting elephant safari, discover traditional village life, and experience the warmth of authentic Sri Lankan hospitality.

Travel through beautiful beaches, lush forests, misty mountains, and green tea plantations. Enjoy a scenic Madu River boat ride, visit a sea turtle hatchery, explore the historic Sigiriya Rock Fortress, and discover the cultural treasures of Kandy and Nuwara Eliya.

🗓️ Detailed Itinerary

Arrive at Bandaranaike International Airport and receive a warm greeting from your dedicated private driver-guide. Transfer to nearby Kurana for rest and traditional Sri Lankan home hospitality.

🤝 Chauffeur Meeting 🏡 Homestay Stay 🍛 Welcome Dinner
Stay: Mr. Amal's Home (1 Night) | Meals: Dinner Included

Travel down to the scenic coastal town of Induruwa. Experience a tranquil boat safari through the mangrove lagoons of the Madu River, visit a sea turtle conservation hatchery, and unwind by the beach.

🐢 Sea Turtle Hatchery 🚤 Madu River Safari
Stay: Pandanus Beach Resort & Spa (1 Night) | Meals: Half Board (HB)

Head to Sri Lanka's vibrant commercial capital, Colombo. Enjoy an immersive city tour exploring historic architecture, modern landmarks, bustling markets, and coastal promenades with time for luxury shopping.

🛍️ Colombo City Tour
Stay: Amari Colombo (1 Night) | Meals: Bread & Breakfast (BB)

Head into the Cultural Triangle of Sigiriya. Embark on an exciting 4x4 jeep safari in Habarana to observe wild elephants in their natural habitat, enjoy a traditional village tour with an authentic home-cooked lunch, and relax with a soothing Ayurveda massage.

🐘 Habarana Elephant Safari 🌾 Village Tour + Village Lunch 💆‍♂️ Ayurveda Massage
Stay: Kassapa Lion Rock (1 Night) | Meals: Half Board (HB)

Climb the legendary 5th-century Sigiriya Rock Fortress in the morning before driving towards the royal hill capital of Kandy. Stop for an authentic lunch at an aromatic spice garden, visit the sacred Temple of the Tooth Relic, and watch a traditional Kandyan cultural dance show.

🏰 Sigiriya 🛕 Temple of Sacred Tooth 🌿 Lunch at Spice Garden 💃 Cultural Show
Stay: Amaya Hills (1 Night) | Meals: Half Board (HB)

Ascend into the misty highlands and tea country of Nuwara Eliya. Pause to admire the stunning Ramboda Falls, followed by a tour of a working tea factory and plantation with a fresh Ceylon tea tasting session.

🌊 Ramboda Falls ☕ Tea Factory & Plantation
Stay: Araliya Green Hills (1 Night) | Meals: Half Board (HB)

Enjoy your final breakfast in Sri Lanka before a comfortable private transfer to Bandaranaike International Airport for your departure flight back home to Hong Kong with wonderful memories of your island adventure.
